quiz Frequently asked questions

Why is the sidebar the same on every screen? expand_more

The sidebar and top bar are injected from a single source of truth in assets/js/app.js, so brand and links stay identical across all tabs.

How do tickets reach the kitchen? expand_more

In POS & Billing, tap "Send to Kitchen". The order is stored in your browser and appears instantly in the Kitchen Display board under "New".

How are tax and service charge calculated? expand_more

Rates come from Settings and are applied to every ticket in the POS. Change them any time — tickets recalculate immediately.

Where is my data stored? expand_more

This prototype stores everything locally in your browser (localStorage) — no account needed. Clearing browser data resets the app.
